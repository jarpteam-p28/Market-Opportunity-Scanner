## Thesis (agents)
**Weekly thesis, 7 Nov 2026**

**Where we are:** The market is mixed and leaning risk-off. SPY is below its 50-day but above its 200-day, VIX is 25.6 and Fear & Greed is 25. This is a megacap and AI drawdown, not a broad rally: the Mag 7 is down 7.7% over a month while equal-weight is up 2.0%. The 10-year at 4.86% sits close to the 5% line. Note that the social-arbitrage analyst's report failed, and Reddit and X data are empty, so talk evidence is thin.

**What is working:** Health Care, Staples and Real Estate lead, and Health Care and Staples have broad participation. Water is the one theme where price and search agree. Moats and efficiency are rising on quiet talk, but that is mostly defensive rotation (UPS and WMT overlap), and "stealth" may just mean missing data. Memory and chips have the loudest talk and the worst prices, so that is capitulation, not an early signal.

**Opportunities** (all low conviction)
1. **Water (AWK, VLTO, WTRG):** Price and search are both up, but there is only one talk source. Utilities sit below their 200-day, so rates are a risk.
   - *Plan:* small starter now, favoring VLTO. Add if the theme holds its 50-day and a second talk source appears. Add on dips only if the cause is market noise. Exit if the theme loses its 50-day or the 10-year hits 5%.
2. **TTWO (GTA 6, Nov 19):** Search is +385% year on year, but the stock is up 13% this week and earnings are Nov 9.
   - *Plan:* no starter before earnings. Humans should read trailer comments first. Consider a starter only after earnings, if the reaction is sound. Exit near launch, when the information is public. A delay breaks the thesis.
3. **ACN (AI users, not builders):** ACN is +26%, but the efficiency-evidence meters are missing.
   - *Plan:* starter only. Add on confirmation that AI is paying off inside companies. Exit if the theme falls below its 50-day.
4. **Memory (MU):** Talk is high, but price is down 24% in a month.
   - *Plan:* watch only. Do not catch the knife. Revisit if price reclaims its 50-day.
5. **CRWV (Nov 10) and NVDA (Nov 17):** These are the canaries for the AI chain.
   - *Plan:* no position. A hold through earnings would support a relief rally.

**Risks that matter most**
- A 10-year at or above 5%, or a daily close below the SPY 200-day, would make this full risk-off.
- The AI chain is breaking all at once (ORCL, CRWV, NVDA, GOOGL, META), so forced selling is plausible.
- Skeptic odds: 45% choppy risk-off, 30% flush then reversal, 25% relief rally.
- WMT has earnings in 12 days, a put/call ratio of 1.44 and is already up 13.5%.

**Sizing:** Low and medium conviction means a small starter position only. High conviction needs several independent sources to agree (price, talk and ground truth) in a supportive regime. If unsure, trim half rather than all.
