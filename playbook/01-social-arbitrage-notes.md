# Playbook notes: social arbitrage (Chris Camillo style), in our own words
- Idea: notice a real change in consumer or public behaviour (what people buy, use, search, complain about or talk about) before it shows up in a company's numbers and before Wall Street prices it in.
- The signal is a CHANGE vs baseline, not a high level. A topic everyone already talks about is usually priced in.
- Confirm with more than one source (social talk + search trends + sales/usage evidence + price starting to react).
- Prefer cases where the trend is big relative to the company (a hit product at a small company moves the stock; at a giant it does not).
- Exit when the information gap closes (mainstream coverage, analysts upgrade, the trend shows up in earnings).
- Our backtest lesson: market regime and drawdown context matter; keep the regime read in every decision.
