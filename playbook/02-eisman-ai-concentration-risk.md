# Notes: Steve Eisman on AI concentration risk (summary in our words)
Source: YouTube interview Pablo shared (Oct 2026). These are the speaker's opinions, not facts we verified.

**Core risk: concentration.** The AI chain (Nvidia → hyperscalers → cloud backlog) depends heavily on a few large AI model labs paying their commitments.
- He cites a large share of Nvidia's receivables coming from a handful of customers.
- He cites a large share of hyperscaler AI revenue coming from OpenAI and Anthropic.
- He cites about half of Oracle's backlog (RPO) tied to OpenAI.
- If a major lab stumbles, he expects the whole chain to slow at once.

**Circular financing.** Much of the labs' funding comes from the same big tech companies and chip makers that sell to them. It holds while growth continues.

**No moats in LLMs.** Users switch models easily. Cheaper open-weight models, including Chinese ones, take share as companies cut "use AI for everything" spending.

**Hyperscalers changed character.** Huge AI capex has eaten their free cash flow (he mentions Google raising equity). He'd be cautious there.

**He prefers "picks and shovels" that receive the cash.** Examples he names: Nvidia, Micron, GE Vernova (gas turbines, backlog years out), Arista, Cisco, Eaton (electrification).

**Power.** US electricity demand is growing again. Data centers are mostly powered by gas turbines; nuclear and on-site fuel cells (Bloom Energy) are alternatives. He says nobody knows how big the power bottleneck really is.

**Software at risk from AI agents.** Travel booking sites and some payments businesses could lose value. Deeply embedded enterprise software looks safer.

**Rates.** Watch the 10-year yield, not the Fed. He sees roughly 5% on the 10-year as the line where the market gets into trouble.

**Oracle as a stress gauge.** Big backlog tied to one customer, high debt, a credit rating near junk, and the stock falling even on good earnings.

## How the scanner uses this
- Flag when the 10-year yield is at or above 5%.
- **Concentration watch:** track Oracle, CoreWeave, the hyperscalers and Nvidia vs the chip index. Breaks there are an early warning for the whole AI chain.
- The skeptic agent should always ask: does this idea depend on AI-lab spending continuing?
